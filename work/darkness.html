Darkness

Archive footage read back from a dark, decaying future.

My film delves into the enigmatic world of human archives, and how hard it becomes to understand their meaning without context in a dark, decaying future. It is a cautionary tale about hate, war and xenophobia today, and how they may manifest in the future if left unchecked.
