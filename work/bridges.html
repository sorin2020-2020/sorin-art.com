Bridges

Places, people and nature in the village of Wassenaar.

Bridges explores the connections between place, community and nature in the village of Wassenaar. Through its cinematography and interviews with local residents, the film looks at the character of this community and how it is shaped by its natural surroundings and by the relationships between its members.
